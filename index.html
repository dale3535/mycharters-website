About MyCharters

Born on the water.
Connected across the islands.

MyCharters began on the water, running private charters around Malta. Along the way we got to know the skippers, the boats and the operators who do this properly, and who doesn't.

Now we put that knowledge to work for you. Instead of one boat, we bring together trusted local operators so you can compare RIBs, motor yachts and catamarans side by side, and book the one that fits your group, your plans and your budget.

You deal with one team from your first question to the moment you step aboard. We pass your plans to the operator, make sure the boat fits the day, and stay on hand if anything changes.

How It Works

Three steps to the water

No phoning around marinas, no guessing which boat is any good. We've done that part.

Browse the fleet

Compare private boats by group size, boat type and trip. Every listing shows what's included, the departure marina and which dates are already taken.

Pick your boat & day

Choose your trip and date, tell us who's coming and any dietary needs, then hold the date with a €100 deposit through Stripe.

Confirm & set sail

The operator confirms your booking and we send you the meeting point. If the boat can't take you, we offer another or refund your deposit in full.
