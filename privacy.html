Privacy Policy

Your data, handled with care.

Last updated 16 November 2026

Who we are

MyCharters is a private boat charter broker in Malta. We arrange charters with independent, licensed boat operators. For anything about your personal data, email mychartersinfo@gmail.com.

What we collect

  • The details you enter in the booking form: name, surname, phone number, address, preferred date, chosen boat and trip, and number of guests.
  • Any allergies or dietary requirements you choose to tell us. This is optional, and we use it only to keep you safe on board.
  • Messages you send us by WhatsApp, phone or email.
  • If you pay a deposit, our payment provider Stripe processes your card details. We never see or store your full card number.
  • Anonymous visit statistics (pages viewed, device type, country). These use no cookies and cannot identify you.

Why we use it

To check availability, send you a quote, arrange and confirm your charter, take your deposit, and contact you about your booking. We do not sell your data, and we do not use it for advertising.

Who we share it with

  • The boat operator running your charter, who needs your name, contact details, group size and any dietary needs.
  • Service providers who help us run the website and bookings: Vercel (website hosting and visit statistics), Stripe (payments), and our email and WhatsApp messaging providers.
  • Authorities, only where the law requires it.

How long we keep it

We keep booking records for as long as we need them to run your charter and to meet our accounting and legal obligations, then delete them. Enquiries that don't become bookings are deleted after 12 months.

Your rights

You can ask us for a copy of your data, ask us to correct or delete it, or object to how we use it, by emailing mychartersinfo@gmail.com. If you are unhappy with how we handle your data, you can complain to Malta's Information and Data Protection Commissioner at idpc.org.mt.
