MyCharters — Terms & Conditions

1. Our Role

MyCharters is a charter broker. We arrange your booking with an independent, licensed boat operator (named on each boat's page and in your booking). The operator provides the vessel, skipper and crew and is responsible for operating the charter safely and in line with Maltese maritime law.

2. Booking Confirmation

A booking is confirmed once the deposit has been received and the operator has confirmed the date, and you receive a booking confirmation from MyCharters. If the operator cannot confirm your date, we will offer you another suitable boat or refund your deposit in full. MyCharters reserves the right to refuse or cancel bookings where necessary for operational, safety, or legal reasons.

3. Payment Terms

  • A deposit (shown when you book, usually €100) is required to secure the booking.
  • The remaining balance is paid as agreed with the operator, normally after the charter unless otherwise agreed in writing.
  • Additional services, fuel surcharges, damages, or overtime may incur extra charges.

4. 24-Hour Cancellation Policy

  • More than 24 hours before departure: full refund of any deposit paid.
  • Less than 24 hours before departure: deposit is non-refundable.
  • No-shows or late arrivals are treated as a cancellation within 24 hours; deposit non-refundable.

5. Food & Beverages

  • Guests must tell us in advance about any allergies or dietary requirements; we pass them to the operator.
  • Neither MyCharters nor the operator is liable for reactions arising from undisclosed dietary conditions.
  • Outside food and beverages require prior approval from the operator.

6. Weather & Force Majeure

The operator or MyCharters may cancel, delay, or reschedule for unsafe weather, mechanical issues, government restrictions or safety concerns. In such cases customers are offered a rescheduled booking or a full refund of any deposit paid.

7. Customer Responsibilities

Guests must follow the skipper's and crew's safety instructions, behave responsibly, and comply with local maritime law. The operator may end a charter without refund for dangerous, illegal or abusive behaviour.

8. Damage & Liability

Customers are responsible for damage caused by their group. MyCharters and the operator are not liable for loss of personal belongings or injuries from failure to follow safety instructions. Participation is at the customer's own risk.

9. Arrival Time

Please arrive at the boat's marina 15–30 minutes before departure. Late arrival may reduce charter time without refund or extension.

10. Acceptance of Terms

By confirming a booking with MyCharters, the customer acknowledges and agrees to these Terms & Conditions.
